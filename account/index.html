How magic link works

  1. 1

    Open the game

    Go to Play and open the Account panel (we open it for you when you use that link).

  2. 2

    Enter your email

    Under Magic link sign-in, type an email you can open right now, then request the link.

  3. 3

    Click the link we send

    Check your inbox (and spam once). Tap the link — you’re signed in. No password. No extra app.

  4. 4

    Use your three slots

    On the title screen, pick a cloud slot for a new run or a continue. Optional: import your local save into a slot.
