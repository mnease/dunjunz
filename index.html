The problem

External

Free games are often ad mazes, installers, or “play later after the shop tutorial.” You just wanted a crawler.

Internal

You want something funny and complete enough to enjoy — without guilt, FOMO timers, or a battle pass breathing on your neck.

Philosophical

Games should be games — not shopfronts wearing a pixel costume. Power you earn. Tips optional. Never pay-to-win.

Your guide

Dunjunz (by NeaseMedia) is parody + homage + chaos: old-man sword tutorials, redshirt misfortune, door riddles that were never subtle, and a princesz who assigns champion jobs like overdue library books.

We’ve been here. We built a browser crawler that respects your time: press play, get a sword, get lost on purpose.
